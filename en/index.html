Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is erased immediately, except the puzzles and box photos added to the shared catalogue, which remain visible without any link to you. Attachments to public events are automatically deleted 3 months after the event date, and published results 12 months after. The purchase identifier and the number of free public events already used are kept to prevent a new account paid with the same subscription from getting them again.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings › Account › “Delete my account”. Deletion is immediate and permanent. Your Premium subscription is not cancelled automatically: cancel it in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, its correction, erasure or portability, or object to its processing, by writing to puzapp.contact@gmail.com. We reply within one month at most. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country of residence (in Spain: the AEPD).

Minimum age

Puz is intended for people aged 16 or over. By creating an account, you confirm that you are 16 or older.

Security

Communication between the app and the server is encrypted. Each player can only access their own data and what their friends share: these rules are enforced by the database itself. Attachments can only be reached from the event page.

Changes

If there is a significant change, you will be notified in the app before it takes effect.

Premium subscription

  • Plans: monthly at €0.99 per month or yearly at €9.99 per year, VAT included. Prices may vary by country; the price shown in the App Store at the time of purchase applies.
  • Free trial: 7 days, for new subscribers only. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the chosen plan is charged.
  • Automatic renewal: the subscription renews at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. Payment is charged to your Apple (or Google) account.
  • Cancellation: at any time, in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You keep Premium access until the end of the paid period.
  • One subscription, one account: a subscription is tied to the Puz account that took it out; it cannot make another account Premium.
  • Refunds: handled by Apple (or Google), under their own terms.
  • End of the subscription: you keep all your puzzles and events, but you can no longer add puzzles beyond 10 or create public events, and Premium features are locked again.

Public events

  • Creation: for Premium members only. The organiser enters the name, date, place (or a link for an online event), rounds, their times, team size and number of places. Each event gets a unique code.
  • Visibility: a public event is offered to players in the chosen region and can be found through search; a private event can only be reached with its code.
  • Price: €0.10 per team, calculated on the total number of places across all rounds, and shown before publishing. The first 3 public events are free per Premium subscription (not per account); deleting an event does not give back a free event. Adding places to an upcoming paid event incurs an extra charge of €0.10 per extra team; removing places (or deleting a round or the event) gives a credit of the same amount, automatically deducted from your next paid events and extra charges until used up. Paid events and extra charges are paid with this credit, which can also be bought in the app in packs of €3, €5, €10 or €20 (one-time payment through the App Store or Google Play). Credit does not expire and cannot be refunded as cash; purchase refunds are handled by Apple (or Google), and a refunded pack is removed from your credit.
  • Organiser’s role: the organiser is solely responsible for their event (accuracy of information, venue, safety, rules, running of the event, compliance with the law) and for approving participants. They alone decide to confirm a registration or put it on the waiting list. Puz provides the tool but does not organise events and is not responsible for them.
  • Registrations: registering with friends also registers them; each one then confirms their participation and can withdraw. A registration is only final once confirmed by the organiser.
  • Attachment: the organiser can attach an image or a PDF (10 MB maximum) that they hold the rights to. It is automatically deleted 3 months after the event.
  • Results: from the day of the event, the organiser can publish the results of each round (image or PDF). They are visible to players who can access the event, confirmed participants are notified, and they are automatically deleted 12 months after the event. The organiser makes sure participants agree to appear in them.

Content you post

The box photos and puzzle information you add (title, brand, number of pieces) join the shared catalogue and are visible to all players. You confirm that you have the right to post them. You grant Puz a free, worldwide licence to display them in the app, including after your account is deleted. The same rule applies to public events and their attachments, for as long as they are kept.

A box photo can be changed for 3 minutes after it is posted, then it is locked. You can report incorrect puzzle information from the app: the administrator may then correct the catalogue. To have inappropriate content removed, write to puzapp.contact@gmail.com.

Rules of use

It is forbidden to post illegal, offensive or non-puzzle-related content, to harass other players, to create fake events or fake registrations, to distort rankings (invented times, multiple accounts), to create several accounts to get around limits or prices, or to try to bypass the service’s security. In case of a breach, the content concerned may be removed and the account suspended or deleted.

Swaps and sales take place directly between players: Puz is not a party to them, collects no payment and is not responsible for the condition of the puzzles, their handover or the payment.
